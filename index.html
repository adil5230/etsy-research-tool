Etsy Research Tool

A small personal tool for studying public Etsy listing data to improve SEO on my own shop.

PERSONAL USE ONLY

Reads public data

Looks at publicly visible listing information such as titles, tags, prices, and favorites counts.

Finds patterns

Compares what successful listings in a niche have in common, to guide my own titles and tags.

Read-only

Does not create or edit listings, send messages, or access anyone's private data.

This tool is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public or sold to anyone.
